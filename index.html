ℹ️ How this works

Adding words: Tell Claude in chat — words sync to this app automatically overnight.

Daily review: Open the app, tap through due cards, rate each one.

Progress: Saved automatically in your browser; with 多设备同步 turned on it is also shared between your devices.

Spaced repetition (SM-2): Again = tomorrow, Easy = weeks away.
